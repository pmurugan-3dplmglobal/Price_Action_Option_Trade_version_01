## 2. Implemented Code Fixes in `trading_core.py`

### Fix A: Bounded Window & Active Swing High Clustering in `find_profit_targets`
- **Location**: [trading_core.py](file:///G:/Poovendan/AI/Trading/Share/ReadyToDeploy/Prod_code_01/Price_Action_Strategy/trading_core.py) -> `find_profit_targets()`
- **Changes**:
  1. Restricted the target scan window to recent active bars (`hist.iloc[-150:]`) to prevent pulling ancient in-the-money option contract highs (e.g. 560.00).
  2. Implemented 5-bar pivot high extraction above `entry_close` with 1.5% cluster deduplication to capture true structural swing resistance levels (e.g. 24.00 & 32.05 for SUNPHARMA, 148.70 & 218.80 for EICHERMOT).
  3. Added safety capping against option price spikes (> 3.5x entry price).

### Fix B: Corrected Stop Loss Offset (`SL = Low - Buffer`)
- **Location**: [trading_core.py](file:///G:/Poovendan/AI/Trading/Share/ReadyToDeploy/Prod_code_01/Price_Action_Strategy/trading_core.py) -> Anchor scanners (`find_anchor_bullish_engulfing`, `find_anchor_ll_sweep`, `find_anchor_hammer_baby`, `find_anchor_bullish_harami`, `find_anchor_two_higher_highs`, `scan_abc_reversal`)
- **Changes**:
  - Replaced the inverse addition `a_low + 2` with proper structural low buffer `sl_val = round(low - max(0.50, low * 0.02), 2)`. This places the Stop Loss safely below the swing low support line, avoiding premature stopouts.
